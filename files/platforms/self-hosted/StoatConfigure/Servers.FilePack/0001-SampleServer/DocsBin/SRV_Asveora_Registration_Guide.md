# Asveora Registration Guide

> We’re excited to see that you want to join the Asveora (asveora.social) network! To begin the registration process we will need to get you setup with a presence within the network. Normally this process begins with looking for a wheelcore to join, but here you have one you can already choose which is `wheelcore.yourwheelcorename.xyz`! So what are the next steps? Simply study the provided diagram and submit a post within <#profile-submissions> in the following format outlined below. Do not include the (00) numbers for each response line. Those are to help you read the diagram. (NI) means the information is not referenced in the diagram. If you have any questions about the format or what certain elements mean, please ask in <#support-mail-house>. Once you have submitted your post, your profile will be generated via quick deployment, and you will receive a confirmation of successful deployment in <#confirm-registration> with further instructions on how to complete your registration with the network!

> **Profile Submission Format**<br>
> > **(NI) Wheelcore: (State the name of the wheelcore you want to join the format of `wheelcore.yourwheelcorename.xyz` here!)<br>
> > **(01) Logo Icon:** (Link or direct upload an image or icon that you want to replace the Asveora icon in the navbar logo. PNGs preferred. JPEGs will be cropped. This is completely optional. Type N/A if you want it to be the standard logo.)<br>
> > **(02) AccentColor1:** (The color or background image of the navbar. See background formatting options section below for more options.)<br>
> > **(03) AccentColor2:** (The color or background image of the ID bar and biography card. See background formatting options section below for more options.)<br>
> > **(04) AccentColor3:** (The color of all title sized text.)<br>
> > **(05) AccentColor4:** (The color of all subtitle sized text.)<br>
> > **(06) AccentColor5:** (The color of all standard sized text.)<br>
> > **(07) AccentColor6:** (The color of link text.)<br>
> > **(NI) Username:** (The readable language username that you want people to find you by via web address. This does not have to be the same as the display name you use for various platforms.)<br>
> > **(NI) 12C-Username:** (The unique identifying 12 character lower case alpha-numeric string that represents you within the network. Use https://www.random.org/strings/ to generate one or make up your own. Make sure that it is not already taken by checking the following index: https://wheelcore.asveora.social/p/usernames.html)<br>
> > **(08) Nameplate Style:** (Name or link of a font that you want your nameplate to be rendered in. For nameplate styling options see the guide below.)<br>
> > **(08) Nameplate Name:** (Only fill out this line if you want your nameplate to show a different name than your username. Think of this as a display name. Otherwise type N/A.)<br>
> > **(09) Bio Title:** (What the title of your biography section will read instead of “My Biography.”)<br>
> > **(10) Bio Body:** (What the text content of your biography reads. See the biography formatting options below for more details.)<br>
> > **(NI) Bio Picture(s):** (Link or direct upload images and GIFs that you want in your biography with numbered tags. Max number of images is 10. See the biography formatting options below for more details.) <br>
> > **(11) Profile Picture:** (Link or direct upload an image or GIF.)<br>
> > **(12) Background:** (Link or direct upload an image or GIF. For a solid color type a color hex code. For more options, please see the background formatting options below.)<br>




> **Background Formatting Options**<br>
> > **Gradient:** If you want to have a gradient background, please submit the color hex codes in a linear order that you want to have seen in it. Linear gradients must be described from their starting point to their ending point for direction. For example a diagonal blue to white gradient would be:<br>
> > `bottom left to top right #0000FF, #A1A1FF, #FFFFFF`<br>
> > where the gradient starts blue in the bottom left corner and ends at white in the top right corner.<br>
> > Radial gradients are calculated from the inside to the outside.<br>
> > **Tinting:** If you want the background image to have a color or gradient tint please state the color or gradient and % of tint which is from 0 (completely transparent) to 100 (completely opaque).<br>

> **Biography Formatting Options**<br>
> >  Use $L, $C, $R to denote a paragraph and determine text alignment as either left, center, or right. Use &BL to denote a blank line. Use [b], [i], [u], or [st] to denote whether the text should be bold, italics, or underlined. Use %T3, %T2, or %T1 for denoting title, subtitle, or standard sized text. Use [IMG#] to denote which image you want inserted. Use <a www.yourlinkhere.com></a> to denote a word as a hyperlink. Images are always displayed on their own line with blank lines on either side. Below is an example:<br>
> > $L This is a left-aligned paragraph with [b]bold text[b] in the middle of it. &BL $C This is a second paragraph that is center aligned with a blank line right before it to separate it from the first paragraph. [IMG01] And here is a third paragraph that has been split from the second paragraph because an image was inserted. And this <a asveora.social>phrase right here</a> is a hyperlink.

> **Nameplate Styling Options**<br>
> > State or link colors, outlines, patterns, and textures that you want your nameplate to have. For example:<br>
> > `Solid #00FF00 with a #FFFFFF outline and polka dot pattern.`<br>
> > which would be solid green text with a white outline and a polka dot overlay or<br>
> > `Bottom to top gradient #0000FF, #FFFFFF with #FF0000 and #000000 outlines`<br>
> > which would be text with a linear blue to white gradient, a red inner outline, and a black outer outline.
