Guidelines

> **GS01.0002 Age and Use of Service Requirements**
> You must be at least 16 years in order to access this community as outlined by the Asveora Constitution. Anyone who is under the age of 16 years is prohibited from accessing this community and if you are discovered to be under the age of 16 years you will be promptly removed from this community and the wider asveora.social network.

> **GS01.0003 Privacy Policy and Best Practices**
> This is a publicly accessible Stoat server with standard verification of entry methods. Any content sent or actions taken within this server are by no means encrypted or obscured. Please keep this in mind as you participate within the community and practice diligent data security. Never post personal or private information relating to yourself, your Stoat account, or the state of another individual. Do not share phone numbers, emails, account tokens, licenses, card numbers, home addresses, citizenship records, passwords, IP dictionaries, or other sensitive information in any capacity for any reason.

> **GS01.0004 Supported Languages and Communication**
> English is the primary language spoken within this community. If you know a second, third, or fourth language, that is terrific! Being bilingual or a polyglot is amazing! However, we ask that you stick to speaking English in order to make community moderation and management easier. Phrases, quotes, or greetings sent in another language are allowed as long as the content of those messages are in alignment with the expectations of this community and they are not the bulk of your communication.

> **GS01.0005 Data Deletion and Account Removal**
> You are free to request that your account and its associated data be removed from the server at anytime for any reason. Do note that if any data associated with your account has been logged for disciplinary action within this community or is being used for an investigation by Stoat, you are entirely responsible for the management of your data and we cannot guarantee removal of the content.



> **GS01.0006 The Administration Is Here for You**

Your membership is highly valuable to this community and we want to ensure that you and everyone else who makes this community what it is has a splendid time. Do not be afraid to talk to figures of the administration or voice your concerns. Even when there are times that administrative figures ask for your compliance to keep everything running smoothly, they are doing so in your best interest and with the goal of keeping you as an appreciated member of the community. Representatives of the administration are instructed to practice grace, civility, understanding, de-escalation, and mutual respect. Those who are violating these virtues will be appropriately addressed. With great power comes great responsibility.

> **GS02.0001 Copyright and Intellectual Property**
> This community is in full support of the preservation of media, information, and contribution. Media shapes culture and culture shapes media. It is the collective expression of the masses. We believe that freedom of information and preserved access to culture is a human right. This is fine and expected for a majority of cases such as sending memes or sharing media from publicly accessible platforms. However, there may be times where you will be asked to remove a piece of content or directed to not re-share a piece of content if it has an elevated possibility of causing legal problems. Refusal to remove the piece of content or comply with directives will result in disciplinary action. This is to ensure that we can avoid problems with those who do not believe in the virtue of preserving culture and broadening access to information.

Membership

> **Membership Classes & Verification**
> There are three classes of membership in this Stoat server since it serves as the community forum of NameOfYourWheelcore’s wheelcore. These classes are Wheelcore Residents, Wheelcore Guests, and External Visitors. Wheelcore Residents are citizens of the Asveora network (known as Asveorans) who are registered to the NameOfYourWheelcore wheelcore while Wheelcore Guests are Asveorans from other Wheelcores and External Visitors are visitors who exist outside of the Asveora Network but are visiting. The verification questionnaire that is required to gain access to the community will help determine which class of membership you fall under.

> **Public Access & Joining Asveora**
> The status of this Wheelcore being open to External Visitors may change based on policies of the NameOfYourWheelcore wheelcore or the security status of the Asveora network. Said status can be found listed in the verification channel. Any verified external visitors already participating within the community prior to changes in access will still be able to interact within the community and treated just like guests and residents. This membership system of the Asveora network was built to allow for the general public to learn about Asveora and potentially become members. If you want to upgrade from being an External Visitor to an Asveoran or specifically an Asveoran that is a part of this Wheelcore, you will be guided through the steps of joining the Asveora network and then re-processed for verification of your new class.
